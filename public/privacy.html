Your privacy in Syncora

Updated 1 November 2026.

Guest access

You can use private rooms without Google sign-in. Supabase assigns a guest identity. Room invitations, nicknames and device playlists may be stored in your browser. Room chat, membership and the shared queue are stored in Supabase. Private room links grant access to available seats, so share them only with people you trust.

Optional Google sign-in

Google sign-in provides basic identity information: name, email address and profile information. Syncora does not request Gmail or Drive access. Supabase manages sign-in sessions. Your joined rooms, personal room nicknames and saved playlists can then follow your account across devices. Other members can see the display name you use in chat; your saved playlists and personal room nicknames are private to your account.

Keeping your guest rooms

When you choose Google sign-in from a guest session, a one-time transfer lets the signed-in account keep that guest’s room seats and host role. The transfer expires after 30 minutes. Its temporary secret stays in that browser tab’s session storage and is hashed in the database. Device playlists are imported only when you choose Import device library.

Storage and removal

Rooms expire after seven days without qualifying activity. Saved account playlists remain until you delete them. Signing out ends this device’s account session; it does not delete your rooms or account library. Release my seat leaves a room. Forget shortcut hides a room shortcut without releasing your seat. Clearing browser storage removes device-only copies and sessions, but does not delete account data.

External services

Syncora uses Supabase for authentication and storage and Cloudflare for hosting and bot verification. YouTube and GIPHY load content directly and apply their own privacy policies. Google handles its sign-in screen. Syncora does not receive your Google password.

Questions or account-data removal

Contact the project owner through the Syncora repository. Do not post passwords, private invitation codes, or personal account details in public issues.
